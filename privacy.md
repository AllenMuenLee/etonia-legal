# Etonia Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Etonia is a handwriting notes app made by Mu-En Lee ("we"). This policy explains
what Etonia collects, why, and what stays on your device.

## Your notes stay yours

Your notes, drawings, PDFs, images and audio recordings are stored on your
device. We do not upload them to our servers and we have no database of note
content.

If you turn on **Google Drive backup**, Etonia copies your notes into your own
Google Drive using the permission you grant. They are stored under your Google
account, not ours. You can turn backup off and delete those files at any time.

## Account

You can write notes without signing in. Even then, the app registers a random
ID created for that installation (not a hardware ID) with our account service
(Supabase), so it knows you are on the free plan.

To use the AI assistant or a subscription, you sign in with Google or, on iPhone
and iPad, with Apple. Our account service then also keeps:

- the email address of the account you signed in with (with Sign in with Apple,
  this can be a private relay address) and an account ID,
- your plan, its renewal and expiry dates, and your AI credit balance.

Sign-in sessions are kept in your device's secure storage (Keychain on iOS).

## The AI assistant

When you ask the AI assistant something, Etonia sends the question, up to the
last 12 turns of that conversation, and an image of only the part of the page
you selected. For drawn answers it also sends the page size and where your
selection sits on it. The whole note is never sent.

The request passes through our server to the AI provider for the model you
chose: Anthropic (Claude models), OpenAI or Google (Gemini). Our server does not
store or log prompts, images, answers or conversations. Each provider handles
the request under its own terms and privacy policy.

We record how much AI you used (token counts and cost) to keep your credit
balance, never what you asked.

## Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and billed by Apple (or Google Play on Android). We do
not see your payment details. We receive and verify the store's record of your
purchase so your plan and credits apply to your account.

## Usage statistics

Etonia sends a short, fixed list of product events, such as "note created" or
"AI request sent", linked to your install ID or account ID. They never
contain note titles, file names, note content, prompts, answers or images. We
use them to understand which features are used and to keep the service working.

We do not use advertising, tracking across other apps or websites, or data
brokers, and we do not sell your data.

## Microphone, camera and photos

Etonia asks for the microphone to record audio with your notes and for voice
input, for speech recognition to turn spoken questions into text, and for the
camera and photo library to place pictures in a note. Recordings and pictures
stay in your notes on your device. Speech recognition is done by your device's
speech service (Apple's on iPhone and iPad), which may process the audio on
its own servers under its own privacy policy.

## Keeping and deleting data

Account, plan and usage records are kept while your account exists. To delete
your account, open **Account**, then **Profile**, and tap **Delete account**.
This deletes your account and its plan, credit and usage records at once, and
revokes Sign in with Apple if you used it. It does not cancel an App Store
subscription; cancel that in your Apple account settings. Uninstalling the app
removes the notes stored on that device.

## Children

Etonia is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect
data from them.

## Changes

We will update this page when these practices change and change the date above.
