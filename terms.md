## Subscriptions

Etonia offers Starter, Pro and Max plans, each billed monthly or yearly through
your App Store account. A subscription renews automatically unless you turn off
auto-renew at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account
is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the period ends. You can manage
or cancel subscriptions in your App Store account settings. Prices are shown in
the app before you buy.

Each plan includes AI credits for its period. Unused credits do not carry over
to the next period and have no cash value.

## The AI assistant

AI answers are generated automatically and can be wrong. Check anything
important before relying on it. Do not use the assistant for medical, legal or
financial decisions.

You are responsible for what you ask the assistant and must not use it to
create unlawful or harmful content.
