# Etonia Support

Etonia is a handwriting notes app for iPad, iPhone and Android, with an AI
assistant that answers in handwriting on your page.

## Contact

Email **[limuen.allen@gmail.com](mailto:limuen.allen@gmail.com)**.

Please include your device model, its system version, and what you were doing
when the problem happened.

## Common questions

**How do I restore my subscription on a new device?**
Open Account, then Subscription, and tap **Restore Purchases**. Sign in with the
same Google or Apple account you used before.

**How do I cancel a subscription?**
Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open Settings, tap your name, then
Subscriptions, and choose Etonia.

**Where are my notes stored?**
On your device. Turn on Google Drive backup in Account, then Backup, to keep a
copy in your own Google Drive.

**How do I delete my account?**
Open Account, then Profile, and tap **Delete account**. Your notes on the device
are kept. Cancel any App Store subscription separately in your Apple account
settings.
